Have a look at this project^^^^.

It uses a cool way of wear leveling in the eeprom. It also doesn't have any ener
gy storage and is powered by an AC wheel hub generator. The counting is done
from counting AC halfwaves of the generator output before it gets rectified into
DC supply for the chip. Also uses an ATtiny85.
